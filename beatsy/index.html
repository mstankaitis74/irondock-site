Put a fingertip on the back camera and Beatsy reads your pulse. Ads never interrupt a measurement, and your data stays on your phone.

What it does

  • Stays on your phone. No accounts, servers or analytics. Measurements are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Camera only while measuring. The picture is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • History with states. Mark a measurement as “Resting” or with a state you create, and export a period as PDF or CSV.
  • Reminders. Scheduled on the phone itself; turn them off in the settings.
  • Health Connect, if you want it. Off until you turn it on. Beatsy only writes there and never reads.
  • Ads on the History screen only. Buying “Remove ads” turns them off.

Beatsy is not a medical device. Results are for information only.

Privacy policy

Questions and answers

How can a phone measure heart rate?

Your fingertip covers the back camera while the flash shines through it. Every heartbeat changes how much light passes through the skin, and Beatsy counts those changes. No watch or chest strap is needed.

Can I use Beatsy for medical decisions?

No. Beatsy is not a medical device. It is for keeping an eye on your pulse, for example at rest or after exercise, and it does not replace a doctor or medical equipment.

Does Beatsy need an account or the internet?

There is no account. Measurements are stored only on your phone. The internet is used for ads on the History screen and for nothing else.

How do I move my measurements to another phone?

Export a period as a PDF or CSV file before you switch. Beatsy data is left out of Android backups on purpose, so it is not copied to a new phone by itself.
