Put a fingertip on the back camera and Beatsy reads your pulse. Ads never interrupt a measurement, and your data stays on your phone.

  • Stays on your phone. No accounts, servers or analytics. Measurements are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Camera only while measuring. The picture is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • History with states. Mark a measurement as “Resting” or with a state you create, and export a period as PDF or CSV.
  • Reminders. Scheduled on the phone itself; turn them off in the settings.
  • Health Connect, if you want it. Off until you turn it on. Beatsy only writes there and never reads.
  • Ads on the History screen only. Buying “Remove ads” turns them off.

Beatsy is not a medical device. Results are for information only.
