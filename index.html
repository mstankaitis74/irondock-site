Irondock (also written Iron Dock) is the name behind a few apps. Each one does a single job, works without an account and keeps your data on your phone. Every app has its own berth on the dock: pick one above.

The apps

Beatsy is a heart rate monitor that reads your pulse through the phone camera. Žalias KET prepares you for the Lithuanian driving theory exam with lessons, tests and every road sign. Talkvault is an automatic call recorder for Android that you download here as an APK.
