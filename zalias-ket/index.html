A clear path to the driving theory exam. The Lithuanian road traffic rules (KET) are split into 26 chapters, from the basics to the exam. The app itself is in Lithuanian.

What it does

  • Learning tree. Each chapter has short lessons with drawings, references to the rules and tips on what people usually mix up.
  • Tricky situations. Over 500 drawn situations: junctions, traffic lights with extra arrows, roundabouts, a traffic officer, level crossings, overtaking and stopping.
  • Every road sign and marking. Almost 300 signs grouped by meaning, with explanations.
  • Tests like the real exam. Exam practice: 30 questions in 30 minutes, 80 % to pass. Plus quick tests, chapter tests and your mistakes repeated after 1, 3 and 7 days.
  • Almost 2,900 questions. And an exam readiness score, so you know when it is time.
  • Games. Sign Lightning, Who Goes First?, True or False and a Daily Challenge.
  • Privacy. The app needs no internet. All data stays on your phone. No ads and no tracking.

The app is not an official product of Regitra or any state institution. The official text of the rules is published at e-tar.lt.

Questions and answers

How many questions are in the Lithuanian driving theory exam?

For category B the exam has 30 questions and 30 minutes, and you need 80 % to pass. The exam practice in Žalias KET uses the same format.

Is the app available in English?

No. The app and all its lessons and questions are in Lithuanian. It covers category B.

Are these the official exam questions?

No. The questions are written for the app from the Lithuanian road traffic rules in force since 1 January 2026, in the format of the real exam. The app is not a product of Regitra.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app needs no internet and no account, and it shows no ads.
